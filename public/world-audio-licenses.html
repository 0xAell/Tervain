Tervain music and sound

The Ford at First Light is Tervain’s original title theme. Its three-beat call returns in the vale, deepwood, spring and nighttime arrangements. The reed, bowed string, struck wire and wooden percussion are instruments written in code.

The game’s environmental beds, material foley, animal calls and character gestures use deterministic synthesis. Characters’ words appear as text. The earlier original Tervain pieces, including Rillford at Work and The Deepwood, use the project’s code-based chamber ensemble.

These recordings are Tervain game content. The repository’s software license does not grant a separate right to reuse the recordings. Archived commercial-service recordings and the 0.1.0 film score have separate historical credits and remain outside the playable soundtrack.
