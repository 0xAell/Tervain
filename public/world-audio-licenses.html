Tervain music and sound

The wanderer and twelve residents use original voices designed from written character descriptions and performed with ElevenLabs. Callum and Harry voice the two toll-jumpers. George narrates the books and notes found in the vale, noticeboard postings, the wagoner’s tally and field observations. Players can also enable George’s readings of menus and interaction prompts in Settings. The cast and narrator are prerecorded. Cast recordings and source credits.

ElevenLabs Sound Effects provided the field, household and wildlife recordings. Three earlier title, riverside and hearthside pieces were produced with Eleven Music from original instrumental briefs. Recording and mastering sources · ElevenLabs terms · Music terms.

The Bell Road has twelve original arrangements and seven stereo environments. Flute, bowed viol, struck wire and tuned glass share a four-bar theme. Wind, water, rafters and village sounds are synthesized from the same editable project. Score and instruments · Track credits.

The Ford at First Light is an earlier original Tervain composition. Its regional arrangements use code-built reed, bowed string, struck wire and wooden percussion. The earlier Rillford at Work and The Deepwood use the project’s chamber ensemble. Original score credits.
