# Tervain 0.1.19 — Wind through the vale

Gusts pass from the Grey Strand into Bellwether Vale. Grass and crop rows lean in waves, while reeds, flowers, shrubs and tree canopies sway at different rates. The grain and vegetable plots show the soil between their rows.

The wreck’s torn sail flexes at the mast, the Hegemony banners ripple outdoors, and indoor wall hangings stir in the draft. Reflections use the same animated poses as the scenery.
