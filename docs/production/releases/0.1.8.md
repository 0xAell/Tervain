# Tervain 0.1.8 — The High Banks of Bellwether

Above the Grey Strand, the sand rises in shelves. Follow them inland to see the quarry drop away below your feet. From the bridge at Rillford, you can see the pebbles through the current and the reflection of the raised roofs along the bank. [Play Tervain](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- The coast, forest paths and quarry have triangulated ground. Sunlight catches the top of a ledge while its face shows bands of exposed stone. The camera follows your elevation, foreground ridges hide the lower parts of trees and figures, and crossing a steep slope changes your stride. Sheer faces send you looking for a gentler way up.
- Houses have solid roof pitches, walls, masonry footings and entrance treads. Roofs and trees cast shadows onto the slope beneath them. The riverbed sits below the moving surface, with gravel visible through the shallows.
- Inside, flights of four steps connect the entry hall to rooms at different heights on each storey. Windows have recessed frames and cast divided patches of daylight. Furniture and residents cast shadows from windows and nearby lamps or hearths.
- Clicking a hillside, bridge deck, entrance tread or raised room directs the wanderer onto its visible surface. Existing journeys reopen at the appropriate floor height.
