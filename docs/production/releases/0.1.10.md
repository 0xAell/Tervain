# Tervain 0.1.10 — Within Earshot

The dog in Rillford answers when you pet it. You can stand by it, check your pack or change a setting in silence while the river and the village carry on around you. [Play Tervain](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- Residents speak when you approach or start a conversation. The wanderer speaks the reply you choose. You can move between conversation choices without interrupting either speaker.
- Books, journal entries, noticeboard postings and field observations have narration. **V** or **Read aloud** replays or stops the current page or dialogue line. Item descriptions and the title-screen introduction can be read on request. Spoken audio settings offer characters and narration, characters only, or off.
- Timber rooms give voices and footsteps a short, soft reflection. Stone interiors return a brighter, longer echo. Music and ambient sound ease down as a spoken recording starts and rise again when the words finish.

The room sound draws on the way *The Last Page* sends sounds along a few paths with different lengths and gains. Tervain synthesizes its own stereo reflections for the materials in the vale. [The Last Page’s room audio](https://github.com/ChetasLua/the-last-page/blob/main/audio/rooms.py).
