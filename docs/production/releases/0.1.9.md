# Tervain 0.1.9 — Loose Cargo

A jug sits on a crate at the Grey Strand. Pick it up, turn it in your hands, and find a new home for it. You can carry it into a house, climb to the loft and put it on a desk. It will still be there when you return. [Play Tervain](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- Crates stack, barrels roll, and loose stones, firewood and household goods have individual masses and collision shapes. Push them by walking into them, drag heavy tables and benches, or use a crate as a step. Seats can still be used after moving them.
- Timber floats and drifts in the river. Denser cargo sinks to the bed. Moving cargo sends ripples across the surface, and brushing or shaking nearby plants sets their branches moving.
- Lift or drag with **G**, turn with **R** or the wheel, and let go with **G**. Click a nearby surface to place an object. **Space** or the right mouse button tosses a light object or shoves heavy furniture. On a controller, use **Y**, **RB** and **X** respectively.
- Loose objects keep their position, orientation and storey when saved. Wood, stone, pottery, metal, cloth and foliage have individual contact sounds. The reader voices the new object names and controls.
