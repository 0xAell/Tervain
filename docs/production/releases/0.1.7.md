# Tervain 0.1.7 — Voices of Bellwether Vale

Joss greets you in his own voice. Choose a reply to hear the wanderer answer, or ask a resident about the vale to hear what is on their mind. The game keeps their words on screen. [Play Tervain](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- All twelve residents and the wanderer have individual performances. Nearby residents can call out or talk among themselves. The two toll-jumpers have distinct voices, and the Thornback gives a warning growl.
- Books, the journal, items, menus and nearby interactions have narration. Press V or select **Read aloud** to read or stop the current page. In play, the same control reads the place, room, time, health, stamina and nearby prompt. Settings lets you choose full narration, character voices or silence.
- Music and environmental sounds ease between locations and quiet down under dialogue. The title, household interiors and riverbanks have new instrumental music. Pets, tools, footsteps, cooking, archery and swimming have sounds tied to movement or contact.
- Speech and sound are part of the game download. The standalone edition plays them offline. [Audio credits](../../../public/world-audio-licenses.html).
