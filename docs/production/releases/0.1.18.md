# Tervain 0.1.18 — Steadier loose objects

Placed barrels, crates, furniture, and dry stones stay where they were set until you touch or move them with enough force. Walking straight into light cargo gives it a short, grounded push. Click routes go around cargo, and heavy furniture moves when you drag or shove it.

Held objects stop at walls and other objects. Let go to drop a load straight down, or use the toss control to send it where you aim. Crates still stack, impacts move other cargo, and water carries floating objects downstream.
