# Tervain 0.1.22 — Light on the road

Cloud shadows drift inland from the Grey Strand. The sand gives way to meadow along the dunes, and wheel tracks follow the wider cart roads. Dusk warms the valley before the ground falls into blue shadow. Cookfires, street lanterns and lit thresholds cast pools of light after dark.

Rillford’s windows light up as evening falls. Switch off the ground-floor lamps and the panes go dark outside. Roofs and canopies shade the ground from nearby lamps.

The view gives the wanderer a little room ahead while travelling or aiming. Footsteps follow the pace of the animation. Running kicks up grit, and alternating bootprints linger in sand and soft earth. A sword follows the wanderer’s hand through the swing and guard. Blows connect on the contact frame, when an opponent can still be in reach or have stepped clear.

The belt keeps all ten bindings visible in one rail. Interaction prompts show the bound key beside the action.
