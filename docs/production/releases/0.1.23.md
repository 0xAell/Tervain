# Tervain 0.1.23 — Stride and impact

A run across the Grey Strand keeps its pace through a jump. All seven jump poses follow takeoff, ascent and landing. Tap X or the left trigger in the last 80 milliseconds before touchdown to spring off again. Land on soft ground and both boots leave a mark. Short clicks now let you adjust your position by less than a single stride.

Takeoff has a short lift of air. A dry landing pairs the sound of the surface underfoot with a low thump. Landing in water makes one splash. Sword swings, guarded blows and confirmed hits each have their own sound. A close hit briefly lowers the music and ambience so you can hear where it lands.

With a bow drawn, the dotted guide follows the arrowhead’s path to a target or obstruction. You hear the ground impact when an arrow strikes terrain or a wall. A sword connects when its blade reaches the contact pose, and a late second press queues the next swing. Damage numbers stay visible above the action after the sparks have faded. Your health bar responds on the frame you take the hit.
