# Tervain 0.1.11 — In-world voices

Open the weaver’s pattern book in her loft and you can hear its words. Later, you can look up that same passage in your journal in silence. [Play Tervain](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- The title screen, journal, inventory and item descriptions stay silent. You can browse while a character finishes talking.
- Conversations, chosen replies, books and notes you find in the world, noticeboard postings and field observations have voiceover. **V** replays or stops an open book, note or conversation.
- The dog still reacts when you pet it. Footsteps echo indoors, and the voice, effects and music settings carry over from saved preferences.
