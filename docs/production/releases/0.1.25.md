# Tervain 0.1.25 — Steel at the ford

Equip the rusted sword and raise guard just before a blow lands. A clean parry catches the blade with a rising metallic ring and opens a short counter. Attack during that opening for a 44-damage riposte. Tap attack during recovery to follow through with a return cut. The overhead swing, return cut and thrust have different wrist and blade paths in all eight directions. A held guard still absorbs most of a blow. Raise guard with **Q**, the controller’s **left bumper**, or the **Guard** touch control.

The encounter display names the opponent and shows health, incoming strikes and the remaining counter window. Warning rings close at the enemy’s feet. A pale outline keeps the wanderer visible behind the quarry ridge, and a copper outline marks the engaged opponent. A steep ledge or building can now stop a melee strike. The soundtrack gives the caught blade, counter release and confirmed hit separate accents. Opening and closing a menu each have a short, dry cue.

Rillford’s roofs, trees and banners stay solid as you walk past. A pale rim traces the part of the wanderer that actually passes behind a roof, canopy, stair rail or piece of furniture. Gaps between branches still show the character’s own colours. Doorstep and interaction marks respect the same foreground as the objects they belong to.

As you wade out from shore, the waterline climbs from boots toward the waist. Short reversals at the waterline no longer play a pair of splashes. The camera and body follow the climb between stair treads. Doors have their own sound, while changing floors lands on wood or stone. Standing up from a seat and reaching for a nearby object ease back into walking.

At dusk the net mender and the woman by the fire stand up and walk to the doors of the net store and the fisher’s house. Their stools stay in place. Inside, residents cross the room to their next post or the stairs, and their conversation target travels with them. Willow and alder roots stay planted in the wind. House shadows track the sun between clock ticks. Lamplight from neighbouring fixtures blends across the floor.

A light tilt of the movement stick gives a slow walk through town or a chamber. Loose stones have four permanent silhouettes, including low slate and split stone. Wide views retain distant loose objects at their saved positions and water depth. Turning a jug or a stone keeps its painted face continuous. Slopes use the same terrain depth on both sides of a streaming boundary.

## QA

In a fixed-camera comparison, the previous cottage changed 4,445 pixels outside the two character positions. The oak changed 1,552. Both measured zero in this patch. A separate check switched the character outline off and on to verify that it remained visible behind each object. The largest vertical step during a 121-position stair traversal fell from 5.14 to 0.68 game pixels.

At 30, 60 and 120 Hz, a late guard stopped the hit and opened one counter. The recorded ford duel uses the game’s own input, HUD and effects. Its stereo track isolates the effects for comparison.

The walkthrough checks covered the coast, Rillford, woodland, the shrine, all three floors of the weaver’s house, sunset departures, combat, the bell, both well responses, inventory, journal, map and quick save/load. The game was also exercised at 2560 × 540, 1280 × 720 at fractional scale, and 393 × 852 at triple density with reduced motion enabled.

To repeat the cottage, oak, interaction-marker and quarry-ridge pixel checks against a running production preview, run `node tools/vale-continuity.mjs http://127.0.0.1:4173/` with Chrome installed.
