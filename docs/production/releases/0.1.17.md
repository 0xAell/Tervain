# Tervain 0.1.17 — Clear roads and banks

Trees grow away from roads, door paths, bridges, beaches, and cultivated ground. Willows and alders stand back from the river’s flood margin. Deepwood has irregular forest edges, open glades, and tall fir stands in sheltered ground.

The world map uses the vale’s actual shoreline, river channels, elevations, roads, bridges, buildings, and tree placements. Forest cover fades into meadows and rock, and the main Hegemony standards remain easy to find.
