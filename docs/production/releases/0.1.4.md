# Tervain 0.1.4 — The Bellwether riverlands

Follow the water from the wreck on the Grey Strand to Rillford’s timber bridge in this isometric pixel-art edition of Bellwether Vale. [Play Tervain in your browser](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- Ocean swells break on the beach. Inland, the sluices change the depth and speed of each channel. The current bends reflections and carries wakes around bridge piers and rocks.
- Trout scatter when you wade into a shoal. Kingfishers and dragonflies visit the planted banks, and small flashes of light drift across the shallows.
- The quarry road crosses the arched wooden bridge from bank to bank. Houses sit on the local terrain, and their doorways open onto furnished rooms.
- The valley, its inhabitants and their animations use custom pixel art. Walk with WASD or click a destination. Press E to interact, Space to attack, X to jump and Esc to pause.
