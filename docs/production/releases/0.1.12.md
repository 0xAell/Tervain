# Tervain 0.1.12 — The wagon road

Bring a strip of salvaged iron to the wagon on the overlook and read Joss’s tally. The wagoner pays four coin for the near wheel. His account and your journal remember the delivery.

- Joss talks about work and the road. Mara answers for the people she has to feed. A daylight PixelLab painting opens the journey at the mill road.
- Quarry tools, flour sacks and herbs sit on worktables in the valley. The mill and quarry sound different as the water allocation changes who can work.

Players who want spoken menus and interaction prompts can select **Characters, world and interface narration** under **Settings → Spoken audio**. The choice is saved on the device. [Play Tervain](https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/).

- Interface narration is off by default for new players and existing saves. Conversations, books and field observations keep their usual voices.
- With the reader enabled, approaching the dog announces the interaction. You can hear journal entries, inspect an item to hear its description, or press **V** to read a page or check your surroundings.
- To switch the reader off, choose **Characters and world narration**. The reader stops immediately, including any queued lines.
