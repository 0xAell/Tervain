# Tervain 0.1.15 — Breaking water

Waves break on the seaward faces of rocks, scatter spray and send reflected ripples back across the swell. River current splits around stones and timber piers, leaving crescents of foam and eddies downstream.

Floating timber follows the swell, bumps into rocks and bridge piers, and makes a splash when dropped into water. Swimmers and walkers go around exposed rocks. Large quarry boulders also block movement and catch loose objects.
