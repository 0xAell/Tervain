# Tervain 0.1.16 — Hegemony standards

The Hegemony flies a gold gate above two river bars across Bellwether Vale. Claret civic standards stand at gathering places. Mulberry watch flags mark bridges, headlands, and the mountain cut. Oxblood pennants line the roads, and short hangings mark civic façades.

The cloth moves in the wind and the freestanding standards cast shadows. Gold pennants on the world map mark the main standards.
