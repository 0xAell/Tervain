# Tervain 0.1.13 — Household stairwells

All 22 houses have full-height flights between the ground floor, loft and cellar. Timber stairs climb to an upper gallery. The stone cellar steps disappear beneath the hall floor, with a lantern set into the stairwell wall.

Walk the steps, click the marked entrance, or press **E** beside it. You can stop on a tread, turn back, or save halfway through a climb. Loose objects rest on the individual steps.
