# Tervain 0.1.24 — Village and woodland

A slow walk keeps the scenery moving between the original art pixels. The same fractional pan carries through the square, along the riverbank and inside larger rooms. Pitched roofs and branches ease out of view when they cover you. Your character and moving creatures travel between ground positions while their animation cels retain their pixel detail.

Pull the rope at Rillford and the bell swings on its yoke with a bronze strike. Draw the bucket at the well to hear how much water remains. When the ration allows a drink, it restores stamina. Click the notices or approach the board and press the interaction key to read the current village news.

Follow a hanging oil lantern to the strand hearth. Its neighbour warms her hands beside the fire. The net mender works his line with both boots on the ground. Cooking sites have one tripod and one flame. Weathered cedar, slate and straw distinguish village roofs. Pine, fir, birch and oak stands have nine locally varied silhouettes with fixed roots. The slate waymarkers, stone arch and beacon meet the land at their foundations.

Doorsteps pick up a small highlight as you approach. The mill lantern stands to one side of the steps, leaving the entrance clear. The meat, iron offcuts, apples and mushrooms in your pack now match what they represent in the world.
