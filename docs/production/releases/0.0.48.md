# Tervain 0.0.48: the browser prototype and the Unreal seed

Tagged `v0.0.48` on 16 November 2026 as a pre-release, the first since `v0.0.5`. Tervain is still a `0.0.x` browser prototype: the tag marks a milestone, not a promotion. A release is tagged at notable milestones, not at every version; see [versioning](https://github.com/0xAell/Tervain/blob/main/docs/production/versioning.md).

**Play it:** <https://0xaell.github.io/Tervain/>, in a desktop browser with WebGL 2. There is nothing to download. The release is the source at the tagged commit and the build published from it.

## The browser prototype since 0.0.5

- **A place.** One region of 580 m by 340 m: a coast, a wood, a waystation and a village. Every building can be walked into, with furnished rooms and a fire in the hearth.
- **People.** Twelve named residents on full-body rigs with authored motion. They work a trade, sit on benches, speak in recorded and captioned lines, go home and sleep.
- **Animals.** Nineteen animals of eight kinds. Thirteen can be hunted with a bow and skinned, and the saddled deer can be ridden.
- **Water.** One system for the sea, the streams and the spring, with wading, swimming and a view under the surface.
- **Grass and trees.** Blade grass that parts for whoever walks through it, and trees that move in the same wind. The oaks, birches, bushes and orchard trees are generated.
- **Sound.** Adaptive music, footsteps by surface, and the sound of the world by place and hour.
- **Play.** Sword and bow against two kinds of hostile, cargo that can be lifted and thrown, an inventory with equipment and a ten-slot hotbar, a journal, a map, saves, and keyboard and mouse or a controller.
- **Checks.** Every pull request passes a play gate and a check that nothing private is added. Every merge is built, tested and published, and the published build is then loaded and played by a smoke test.

## The Unreal seed

The repository has been reworked so that the game's rules and data can be carried to Unreal Engine 5.9 and later. The [README](https://github.com/0xAell/Tervain/blob/main/README.md#repository-map) maps it, and the [roadmap](https://github.com/0xAell/Tervain/blob/main/docs/unreal/roadmap.md) sets out the port in phases.

- **The engine-agnostic core** (`src/core`). State, content, rules and world layout in TypeScript with no renderer, browser or package dependency, behind a boundary CI enforces.
- **Portable data** (`data/`). The core's tables as JSON with schemas, the terrain as Landscape rasters, and DataTable-shaped files in Unreal's units.
- **The asset manifest** (`data/assets`). Every model, texture and sound with its source, its license, and whether a port keeps, replaces or drops it.
- **The porting docs** (`docs/unreal`). How each system maps to Unreal, the conventions, what the engine's web target can do, and the roadmap.
- **The C++ skeleton** (`unreal/`). A source-only project that mirrors the core's data types. CI compiles, links and runs it against stand-ins for the engine's headers. It has never been built by the engine.

## Not in it

- Dialogue choices. The first quest exists as rules, data and tests, not as something you can play to its end.
- A desktop package, or anything to do with a store release.
- Final art. Most models and building surfaces, and the title music, are stand-ins.
- Measurements on reference hardware.

The earlier releases (`v0.0.4`, `v0.0.5`, `v0.0.5-npc`) are kept as they were.
